Questions before ordering

Reman auto FAQ

What information should I have before calling?

The exact OEM/MPN or engineering number is most useful. VIN plus year, make and model can provide a second application check.

Does every reman unit require a core return?

No. Core requirements vary by product and source. Any deposit, timing and return responsibility are confirmed before purchase.

Are all electronic reman units plug-and-play?

No. Some units may require coding, programming or relearn procedures. Any known requirement should be confirmed for the exact application before ordering.

Is warranty the same on every reman unit?

No. Warranty coverage varies by remanufacturer and product. Applicable terms are confirmed for the specific unit.
