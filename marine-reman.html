Questions before ordering

Reman marine FAQ

What does “remanufactured” mean on this page?

Remanufactured or rebuilt condition is described according to the specific product or supplier documentation. The exact condition description is confirmed before purchase.

What information should I have before calling?

The exact part number is most useful. Engine brand, model and serial number can provide an additional application check.

Will every reman marine part have a core charge?

No. Core requirements vary by component and source. If a core deposit or return is required, the amount, return timing and responsibility are confirmed before the order is finalized.

Is warranty coverage the same for every reman unit?

No. Warranty coverage varies by remanufacturer and product. Applicable terms are confirmed for the specific unit before purchase.
