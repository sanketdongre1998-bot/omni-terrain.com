Omni Terrain US policy

Privacy Policy

This notice explains how PRP Xpert LLC, operating the Omni Terrain United States storefront, collects, uses and shares information when you browse, sign in, request support or place an order.

Effective 3 September 2026US storefrontPRP Xpert LLC

Information you provide

We may receive your name, email address, telephone number, billing and shipping address, product selections, fitment or order notes, support messages and business correspondence. Stripe receives payment details during checkout. Omni Terrain does not receive or store your full payment-card number.

Account information

If you choose Google sign-in, Firebase Authentication receives your Google account profile name, email address, profile image and a unique account identifier so the Omni Terrain account and sign-in session can be maintained. Google sign-in is optional and is not required for browsing the catalogue.

Device & attribution data

The site may process IP address, browser or device information, pages and products viewed, cart actions, referring URL, UTM campaign parameters and advertising click identifiers such as gclid, gbraid or wbraid. Browser local storage is used for the cart, theme preference, account session, purchase-event deduplication and first/last traffic attribution.

How information is used

Information is used to provide the site, maintain accounts and carts, validate products and checkout, process and fulfill orders, communicate about delivery or returns, answer enquiries, prevent fraud or misuse, measure store and campaign performance, maintain records and meet legal, tax and security obligations.

Service providers

Information may be shared as reasonably necessary with Stripe for payments, Google/Firebase for optional account sign-in, website hosting and measurement providers, email and support services, suppliers, warehouses, carriers, fraud-prevention providers and professional advisers, or when required by law. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy notices.

Sale, advertising & choices

We do not sell personal information for money. Campaign identifiers and store events may be used to understand whether an advertisement led to a visit or purchase when a measurement service is enabled. You can clear local storage or use browser privacy controls; doing so may sign you out, clear the cart or reduce measurement. Where applicable law provides additional access, correction, deletion or opt-out rights, you may request them using the contact below.

Retention & security

Information is retained only as reasonably needed for accounts, orders, support, accounting, dispute prevention, security or legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no website, email or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Contact & requests

Send privacy questions or verified rights requests to support@omni-terrain.com. Include enough information for us to identify the relevant account or order. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

The U.S. storefront is operated by PRP Xpert LLC, 30 N Gould St Ste R, Sheridan, Wyoming 82801. This notice may be updated when store providers or practices change; the effective date above identifies the current version.
