UK Privacy Policy

This policy explains how PRASAD INC LTD, trading as Omni Terrain, uses information connected with the UK website, customer support and orders.

PRASAD INC LTDCompany No. 07981226Last reviewed 11 August 2026

Information we may receive

Depending on how you interact with us, we may receive your name, email address, telephone number, delivery and billing information, product or order details, support messages and technical website information such as IP address, browser type and requested page.

How information is used

Information may be used to answer enquiries, process and fulfil orders, provide delivery updates, manage returns and refunds, prevent fraud, maintain business records, comply with legal obligations and improve customer support.

Payment information

Do not send full card details by email. Current UK marketplace payments are processed through the marketplace checkout.

When direct website card payments are enabled, card information will be entered into the secure payment flow provided by our payment service provider. PRASAD INC LTD does not intend to store full card numbers or card security codes on this website.

Payment service providers

Direct website card payments may be processed by third-party payment providers such as Stripe. Those providers process payment information under their own security and privacy terms and may provide us with payment status, transaction references and limited billing information needed to administer the order.

Marketplaces & fulfilment partners

Where an order is placed through an external marketplace or fulfilled using a supplier or carrier, information needed for checkout, payment, fulfilment, tracking, support, fraud prevention and returns may be processed by the relevant marketplace, supplier, carrier or service provider.

Retention & security

We keep business and customer information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected, including support, accounting, tax, fraud-prevention and legal record-keeping requirements. Reasonable organisational and technical measures are used to protect information we control.

Privacy contact

Questions about personal information can be sent to support@omni-terrain.com.
