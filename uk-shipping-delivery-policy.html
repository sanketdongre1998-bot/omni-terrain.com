UK Shipping & Delivery

Current Shield Autocare products include free standard delivery to standard UK mainland addresses. Other UK delivery areas must be confirmed before payment so any applicable surcharge and delivery method are clear.

Current fulfilment route

Current UK catalogue products are supplier-backed physical goods. Depending on the item, fulfilment may be handled from the supplier or distribution location used for that product. Tracking and applicable delivery details are provided with the order.

Delivery estimate

Delivery estimates can vary by product, stock location and destination. For the current Shield Autocare range, standard UK mainland delivery is normally the standard service; any faster or destination-specific service is subject to availability.

Delivery charges

Current Shield Autocare products include free standard delivery to standard UK mainland addresses. Scottish Highlands and islands may carry a £10 surcharge where applicable. For Northern Ireland, Isle of Man, Channel Islands or another remote-area service, contact support before ordering so the applicable delivery charge and eligibility can be confirmed.

Destinations

The current supplier can service UK mainland and a number of non-mainland UK destinations, but the free-delivery checkout route is currently limited to standard UK mainland addresses. Customers in Highlands, islands, Northern Ireland, Isle of Man or Channel Islands should contact support before payment.

Supplier-specific shipping

Shipping rules can differ between product ranges and suppliers. A future automotive, marine or electrical range may use a different delivery charge or fulfilment rule. The applicable delivery terms for that product will be shown before payment rather than automatically applying the Shield Autocare delivery rule.

Tracking

Where tracking is available, tracking information is provided through the order record or by the relevant carrier after dispatch. Tracking can take time to become active after the label is created.

Delays

If an order is delayed beyond the stated estimate, contact support@omni-terrain.com. We will review the latest carrier or fulfilment information available.

Damage, shortage or wrong item

Report visible transit damage, missing items or an incorrect product promptly. Keep the packaging, shipping label and photographs where relevant. Do not permanently install a visibly damaged or incorrect product before receiving guidance.
