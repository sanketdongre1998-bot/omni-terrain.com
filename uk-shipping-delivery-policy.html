UK Shipping & Delivery

Delivery cost, destination eligibility and the estimated delivery window are shown to the customer before payment wherever an order can currently be placed.

Current fulfilment route

Current UK catalogue products are supplier-backed physical goods. Depending on the item, fulfilment may be handled from the supplier or distribution location used for that product. The order confirmation or marketplace checkout provides the applicable delivery details.

Delivery estimate

The exact estimated delivery date or delivery window shown before payment should be treated as the current estimate for that order. We do not publish one universal delivery time because product size, stock location and destination can differ.

Delivery charges

Any delivery charge or surcharge is shown before payment. Customers should review the final order total and delivery method before completing checkout.

Destinations

Destination eligibility can vary by product. UK mainland delivery may differ from Highlands, islands, Northern Ireland or other remote-area services. If a destination is not supported by the checkout shown for the product, contact support before attempting to order.

Tracking

Where tracking is available, tracking information is provided through the order record or by the relevant carrier after dispatch. Tracking can take time to become active after the label is created.

Delays

If an order is delayed beyond the stated estimate, contact support@omni-terrain.com or use the relevant marketplace order thread. We will review the latest carrier or fulfilment information available.

Damage, shortage or wrong item

Report visible transit damage, missing items or an incorrect product promptly. Keep the packaging, shipping label and photographs where relevant. Do not permanently install a visibly damaged or incorrect product before receiving guidance.

Checkout information: Delivery method, charge and estimate will be shown before payment through the active checkout route. Orders placed through an external marketplace use the delivery information shown in that marketplace order.
