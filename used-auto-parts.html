How used OEM buying works

Used parts need a different customer flow from new catalogue products, so the storefront keeps the information separate and explicit.

1

Match the number

Start from the OEM/MPN printed on the original component, not only the vehicle description.

2

Confirm vehicle details

Year, make, model, trim and options are used when interchange or supersession needs review.

3

Review condition

Live listings should show condition notes and actual product photos whenever available.

4

Ship & support

Handling time, delivery terms and returns are shown before purchase for each live item.

Used vs new: Used OEM inventory is intentionally kept separate from Omni Terrain’s new-parts catalogue so condition, donor information and part-number matching stay clear to the customer.
